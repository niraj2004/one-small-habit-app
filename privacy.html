OSH Analytics privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who uses this app

OSH Analytics is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel One Small Habit, on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through those services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own channel to:

  • upload and schedule videos, and set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails;
  • post comments as the channel;
  • read the channel's videos and statistics, such as views, watch time and subscribers.

It also reads public information about other YouTube videos, such as titles and view counts, to see which topics are popular.

How the information is used and stored

The information is used only to run and improve the One Small Habit channel. Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored on the owner's own computer. The app uses no cookies and does not collect information from people who watch the channel.

The information is not sold. It is shared only with the services needed to do this work: Google, to carry out actions on the channel, and the AI assistant the owner uses to run the tool, which processes these statistics to help plan videos.

Removing access

The owner can remove this app's access at any time on the Google security settings page. Statistics stored on the owner's computer can be deleted there at any time.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the One Small Habit channel page.
